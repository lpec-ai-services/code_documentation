# Volume

**Volume** mesh quality criterion reflects the volume of meshes of a 3D object.

*To apply the Volume quality criterion to your mesh:*

1. Display your mesh in the viewer.
2. Choose **Controls > Volume Controls > Volume** or click *“Volume”* button ![img](https://docs.salome-platform.org/latest/gui/SMESH/_images/image145.png) in the toolbar.

   Your mesh will be displayed in the viewer with its elements colored according to the applied mesh quality control criterion:

   > ![figure](https://docs.salome-platform.org/latest/gui/SMESH/_images/image143.gif)

**See Also** a sample TUI Script of a [Volume](tui_quality_controls.md#tui-volume) filter.
