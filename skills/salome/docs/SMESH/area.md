# Area

**Area** mesh quality control is based on the algorithm of area calculation of mesh faces.

*To apply the Area quality control to your mesh:*

1. Display your mesh in the viewer.
2. Choose **Controls > Face Controls > Area** or click **“Area”** button ![img](https://docs.salome-platform.org/latest/gui/SMESH/_images/image35.png).

Your mesh will be displayed in the viewer with its faces colored
according to the applied mesh quality control criterion:

> ![figure](https://docs.salome-platform.org/latest/gui/SMESH/_images/image5.jpg)

**See Also** a sample TUI Script of an [Area](tui_quality_controls.md#tui-area) filter.
