This publication provides a comprehensive review of the current status
of research and development in the field of thermohydraulics for
innovative fast neutron reactors. It summarizes the work presented by
the participants at the Technical Meeting on State-of-the-art Thermal
Hydraulics of Fast Reactors (26–30 September 2022) and serves
as reference material for experts in the field. Within the scope of the
technical meeting and its related topics, this publication promotes and
facilitates the exchange of information at both national and international
levels. By identifying gaps in research and development, it is a key
resource for assessing future requirements and concentrating attention
on key areas.
