Part 1: Principles and modeling
Heat conduction, convection, and radiation are the major mechanisms we encounter in
heat transfer phenomena in nuclear thermal hydraulics that are a subject of many
books and articles in professional journals. This chapter is dedicated to the fundamental
aspects of engineering thermal hydraulics of nuclear reactors, being based on the
well-established disciplines, rather than recent cutting edge type new technology
